About Gafat Art

Gafat Art Painting and Design Works is a creative practice centered on painting and visual design — exploring color, form, and craftsmanship across a growing body of original work.

Every piece in this gallery reflects an ongoing pursuit of artistic expression and visual storytelling, from concept through finished canvas.
